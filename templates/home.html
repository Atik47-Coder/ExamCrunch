Privacy first

Asking for help should never feel embarrassing.

Nobody sees who needs help. We built the rules into the code, not just the screen.

  • Ratings are private. The class sees only counts.
  • Counts stay hidden until 3 classmates have rated a topic.
  • Ask anonymously. Classmates and your helper see "Anonymous student".
  • Study groups are opt-in. You join only when you press Join.
  • Shared files can be downloaded by course members only.
